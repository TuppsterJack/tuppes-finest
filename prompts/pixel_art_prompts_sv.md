# Promptmallar för pixel art-produktion

## 1) Hero base (från uppladdad referens)
"Create a 32x32 pixel-art character sprite sheet based on the provided reference. Preserve silhouette, hairstyle, and outfit identity. Top-down action RPG style, readable at 1x scale, 4-direction set (N,S,E,W), transparent background, no anti-aliasing, clean clusters, restrained palette."

## 2) Hero animations
"Generate animation rows: idle(6), walk(8), run(8), attack_1(8), attack_2(10), hurt(4), jump(6), fall(2), death(10). Ensure timing arcs and impact anticipation frames."

## 3) Slime mob
"Create a 32x32 slime enemy sheet: idle(4), move(6), attack(6), hurt(3), death(6). Color variant ready for palette swaps (green, blue, red)."

## 4) Skeleton mob
"Create a 32x32 skeleton enemy sheet: idle(6), walk(8), attack(8), hurt(4), death(8). Medieval dungeon fantasy tone."

## 5) Bat mob
"Create a 32x32 bat enemy sheet: fly(6), attack(6), hurt(3), death(6). Keep wing readability in dark cave backgrounds."

## 6) Brute mob
"Create a 48x48 brute/ogre sheet: idle(6), walk(8), smash(10), hurt(4), death(10). Heavy weight animation with strong anticipation."

## 7) Warrior idle/auto pack
"Create 32x32 warrior animation sheet for idle game states: idle(6), auto_walk(8), auto_attack(8), cast_start(4), cast_release(6), aoe_cast(10), hurt(4), death(10), victory(6). Emphasize heavy melee arcs and strong impact frame for Whirlwind release."

## 8) Mage idle/auto pack
"Create 32x32 mage animation sheet for idle game states: idle(6), auto_walk(8), auto_attack(7), cast_start(5), cast_loop(4), cast_release(6), aoe_cast(10), hurt(4), death(10), victory(6). Clear casting poses and readable hand silhouettes."

## 9) Archer idle/auto pack
"Create 32x32 archer animation sheet for idle game states: idle(6), auto_walk(8), auto_attack(6), cast_start(4), cast_release(6), aoe_cast(9), hurt(4), death(10), victory(6). Focus on bow readability and snappy release frame timing."

## 10) AOE VFX pack
"Create three 64x64 transparent VFX sprite sheets for AOE spells: Whirlwind(12 frames), Arcane Nova(14 frames), Volley Rain(16 frames). No anti-aliasing, high contrast, clear center origin for engine alignment."
